# multihop_mt audit, 2026-10-15

Triggered by Neel's review, which flagged three items as reachable without their bridges: the
Sogdian-commander item, the Emperor Xuanzong item and the Victorian-noblewoman item. Every one
of the 100 frozen items was probed on Qwen3.6-27B with the bank's own gate recipe: chat
template, `enable_thinking=False`, answer stem `My answer is:`, the fill-in question, 10
samples at T=0.7 plus greedy, scored with `contains_target`.

**Method.** For each item, fresh-context analysts wrote ablation probes, which were then run
on the model. There are two kinds:
- **Bridge removed.** The clue that pins a bridge is genericized and the rest of the prompt is
  kept.
- **Final clause only.** Only the answer-defining clause is kept, with a generic subject.

An item has a **shortcut** when any ablation still produces the target at least 5/10.

**Finding: 93/100 items have a measured shortcut, including all 32 "3hop" items.** The
shortcuts are of two kinds:
- **Specific final clause.** The clause names the target by itself: "the ninth planet found in
  1930 by" → Tombaugh 10/10.
- **Category prior.** The target is the default answer of its class: "the famous American
  racehorse spent his breeding career at" → Claiborne Farm 10/10.

Root cause: the v1 generator asked for "long-tail, obscure-but-real" bridges. Qwen knows only
an obscure entity's single most famous fact, so the final hop had to use that fact, and that
fact is what makes the final clause a shortcut.

Three repair rounds verified 15 clean items. They seed `multihop_mt_v2` (plan 0013), which adds
a shortcut gate. **This bank is frozen and is not edited.**

The table below lists every item's strongest measured shortcut and its verdict:
- `EXCLUDE`: no clean repair Qwen could answer;
- `FIX→2hop`: the verified repair;
- `KEEP`: the original prompt was clean.

Every probe text, together with its samples and scores, is archived in global-workspace under
`docs/project/experiments/multihop_v2/audit/`.
