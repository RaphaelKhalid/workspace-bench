## role_bound_association
- **The gate certifies direction by name, not role inference** (systemic; the same holds on the 0.8B).
  - 94/100 kept greedy answers name only the people ("Priya comforted Marcus.", "Dale interrogated Miriam."), and 43/451 samples name any role word.
  - They pass because the grader rewrites names to "the <role> (<name>)" from the item.
  - This matches the family judge, which accepts people "by name or description", and the scored latent (direction) is stated in the stimulus (prompt-only 1.00 by design). So it is not a bank defect.
  - But the addendum's "0 → 100" is a grading change, not evidence that OLMo-7B infers roles from cues. Don't read the incongruent stratum as stereotype override on this subject.
- `ask_roles` is gate-only; the read plan (stimulus + summarize suffix) is unaffected (verified: the plan never contains the appended sentence).
- Nit (inherited from the frozen bank): `oa-professor-dean-report-ab` gold_label has a trailing period ("the professor reported the dean.").

## brew_intermediates (11 originals + 89 generated, all n_rows 10, depth 2)

- **Correctness:**
  - all 100 colour chains were recomputed from each item's own `table` and, separately, re-parsed from the prompt text. Rules, start and stir count match the table 100/100; intermediate and answer are exact 100/100; every chain has three distinct colours.
  - `options_adjacent` holds {start, intermediate, answer} plus 2 printed off-trajectory colours in 100/100. Every option is printed in the table, and `off_trajectory` equals the recomputed set 100/100.
- **Leaks:** the intermediate never appears outside the rule lines (0/100). It sits in the rules by construction, as in the frozen bank.
- **Dup / skew:** 0 duplicate tables. A start→intermediate→answer signature recurs at most twice. Intermediate colours: teal 25, red 13, green 11 → **teal 25/100 (over the 20% cap)**.
- **Gate recompute:**
  - every kept item's last gate record is greedy-right with 10/10 samples. A deterministic re-grade (first word = answer, intermediate not written) agrees on every greedy and sample text, and gated content equals bank content 100/100.
  - every gate question is the read render: no system turn, prefill `Answer:`.
  - no id collisions in the gate log. Generated items carry a stale `id` field from the generator (`brew-t0-s0-…`), but `banks.load_bank` re-labels from `name`, and the gate log is keyed by `name`.
  - yield: 96/1010 generated candidates (9.5%).
- **Read site:** `brew_port.derive` was re-run with the pinned subject tokenizer (offline) on all 100 items. It equals the stored `regions` 100/100, and the plan positions equal the region cells 100/100, 24 cells per item. Each stir / start / question region's decoded text contains its whole prompt line. Emission cells decode to `assistant` / `\n` / `:` (ChatML without a think block: emit_asst = the role token) on 100/100.
- **Judge** (own judge, `claude-opus-5-5`, emission cells at one layer, 8 items): gold 8/8, foil (an off colour) 0/8, irrelevant 0/8.
- **Latent kind:** unchanged (single mode, depth 2, 10 rows, i.e. T0, the colour after the first stir). Nothing easier than T0 was needed.
- **Observation:** Of 1,010 generated candidates, OLMo-7B passed 28.6% of the teal-intermediate items against 4–13% for every other colour, and 0/98 items whose *answer* is teal. The likely mechanism: the subject avoids saying "teal", so the usual depth-2 error (emitting the intermediate) is suppressed exactly when the intermediate is teal. These items still pass greedy + 10/10 at a 1/12 chance level, so they are valid, but the selection concentrates on one colour, and a lens that always says "teal" passes 25% of the bank (the rule K·G > ΣO is met whenever teal is the gold).
