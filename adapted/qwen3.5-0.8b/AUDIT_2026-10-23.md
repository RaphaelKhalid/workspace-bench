## Findings

**1. poetry: 11 authored items whose gold the subject cannot have planned at the read site (drop).**
- The read site is the newline that ends line 1, so the latent is the rhyme planned from line 1.
- 48/64 authored items (vs 4/36 originals) have a line 2 that Opus marks as an idiom forcing the word. The T1 ladder allows this ("idiomatic or cliché second lines").
- In 11 of the authored items, line 2 has nothing to do with line 1, and the gold is not in Opus's top-5 rhyme plans from line 1 alone. Examples:
  - "The children ran to fly a kite, / The old film played in black and ___" → white;
  - "He'd only worked here a few years, / The boss said he was wet behind the ___" → ears;
  - "The king was rich as rich can be, / He kept his gold under lock and ___" → key.
- The word is fixed only by line 2, which comes after the read position. A lens reading the subject at the newline has no reason to contain it.
- Line-1 top-5 recall: originals 35/36 (the 36th was an empty Opus answer), authored 53/64. Top-1: originals 26/36, authored 33/64.
- → Drop the 11 (list in the JSON). The other 37 idiom items keep a line-1-plannable rhyme. For future waves, steer toward "line 2 continues line 1's scene" rather than any idiom.

**2. role_bound (systemic, also OLMo-7B): the gate certifies a name echo, not role inference.**
- All 100/100 kept greedy answers name the people, never their roles.
- 86/100 greedy answers are ≥ 90% words copied from the stimulus (e.g. "Sana calmed Ben down.").
- Only 14 of 1,000 samples name any role word.
- They pass because the grader rewrites each name to "the <role> (<name>)" from the item itself (`families._role_bound` name_roles). The role binding in the graded text is therefore supplied by the item.
- This is consistent with the family's own judge, which credits people "by name or description" with "Name, the role" option labels. The scored latent is the direction, which the stimulus states outright (prompt-only floor 1.00 by design).
- So it is not a bank defect. But nothing in this bank shows that the 0.8B binds roles from the cues, and the incongruent stratum says nothing about stereotype override for this subject.
- 43/100 kept items have a sampled rate < 0.8. This is allowed (the source gate is greedy-only), but worth knowing.

**3. user_modeling: the macro decision.**
- The 42 synthsys items are originals, not padding. The frozen bank itself is 47 selfdescribe / 53 synthsys and reports `by_subfamily`, so this set's 58/42 is closer to inference than the original.
- Recommend including it in the macro with the subfamily split reported beside the headline, as the frozen bank does.
- Caveats:
  - the gender items are 10 male / 3 female, so a "male" prior scores 77% on that class;
  - the new cues are strong (Opus called all 21 selfdescribe items in its sample "implied", none "stated"), so expect a high prompt-only floor; re-measure the floor before quoting.
- Stem hits are cue words, not the gold: audit→auditor, airliner→airline captain, patrol→patrolman.

**4. typo_mt:** clean. Its status is `partial` (29); keep it excluded from the macro as the report says.
