  - 150/161 items' topics have yes/no-led elicitation draws. The other 11 are all deliberative items (Gemma says "Debatable…" in every draw), where the item carries both a yes and a no reason, so the empty alignment check does not matter.

Quote moral beside its floor, and don't read a pass as evidence for the side.
